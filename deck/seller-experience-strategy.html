01 · The opportunity

Tise has more supply than it converts.

Listings are created too shallow to be discoverable, sellers have no progression ladder once they publish, and inventory goes stale because nothing nudges it to refresh — so the marketplace looks liquid but is not.

What "good" looks like for us — a liquid and relevant inventory: a buyer searching or browsing on Tise consistently finds well-described, fairly-priced, in-stock items from responsive sellers, and a seller publishing a listing has a high chance of selling it within a reasonable window. Liquidity is the rate at which inventory turns into sales; relevance is the fit between what is listed and what buyers actually search for.

A
Listing creation Quality is left to chance
  • Title capped at 20 chars → weak CTR & search recall
  • Description optional, no AI assist
  • Only 4 of 8 photo slots visible upfront — most listings stop at the median 4
  • No price guidance → mispriced inventory
B
Seller tools Infrastructure present, progression absent
  • Dashboard exists — not actionable per listing
  • No response-time or Top Seller signals
  • No bulk-edit / relist tooling
  • No membership tier for power sellers — monetisation & loyalty lever untapped
Evidence is thinner here
C
Inventory density Supply looks deep but doesn't turn
  • No freshness or relist nudge
  • ReTise buried; dormant sellers untouched
  • No quality-aware ranking in feed/search
  • Stale listings consume impressions
Why now Supply-side leverage Better listings lift both Buyer Rate (% of visitors who buy) and AOV (average order value) at the same time
Where the value sits First listing + never-relisted Both inside our scope

North star
Sell-through Rate (30d)

% of newly-published listings that sell within 30 days — the cleanest measure of liquid & relevant inventory.

Operating metric (leading)
Supply Quality Index (SQI)

≥4 photos · brand · size · description ≥80 chars · price within ±30% of category-median comps.

Program north star
GMV MAU

Gross Merchandise Value sold, divided by Monthly Active Users.

Buyer Rate × Orders per Buyer × AOV
Better listings raise card CTR and view-to-intent (Buyer Rate), better-priced and higher-tier listings raise AOV, and a healthier inventory pool sustains both over time.
Driver 1

Listing creation health

  • Create-flow completion · time-to-publish
  • Photos / listing · brand+size coverage
  • AI-assist usage · price-vs-comp deviation
Driver 2

Seller progression

  • Activation · time-to-second-listing
  • Casual → active graduation
  • D30 / D90 retention · response time
Driver 3

Inventory density

  • Active listings / market / category
  • New + reactivated listings / week
  • Stale-listing share · % fresh in last 30d
Guardrail

Must not regress

  • Refund / Not As Described disputes · moderation removal
  • Customer Satisfaction Score · support tickets
  • Time-to-payout

How we get there

Insight (Phase 1) and listing-creation wins (Phase 2) run in parallel. Seller progression (Phase 3) starts once cohort data lands.

1

Instrument & diagnose

Weeks 1 – 3
  • Audit lifecycle tracking end-to-end
  • Baseline dashboard: sell-through, SQI, funnel
  • Define & backfill SQI v1
  • Cohort the seller base by tier
  • Sprig surveys on 3 cohorts
Outcome: baseline, validated SQI, ranked backlog
2

Listing-creation quick wins

Weeks 2 – 6 · parallel
  • Title cap 20 → 60–80 chars
  • AI description assist
  • Photo-count nudge · 8 slots upfront
  • Price-guidance widget
All A/B in Margit testbed with guardrails
3

Progression & density

Weeks 5 – 10
  • Seller Dashboard v2 — public quality score
  • Response-time badges on profile/card
  • Top Seller status + ranking signal
  • Dormant ReTise reactivation loop
  • Bulk-edit tooling · Power-seller membership interviews
Builds the ladder + keeps inventory fresh

Phase tail · Weeks 11–13 — synthesis & recommendation: scorecard of every experiment, re-ranked backlog, and a one-page post-90d bet (Power-seller membership · SQI in ranking · catalogue import).
